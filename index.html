Compliance · EU AI Act · NIS2

Evidence your auditors can read.

Auditors ask two questions about your software: who changed what, and which data went to which AI service. mergecat answers both, with records you can hand over.

Ask before AI sees your code

Before the Claude Code plugin sends any repository data, mergecat asks once per session whether you use a Claude Team or Enterprise account. If not, it asks whether you know the AI Act and NIS2 and want to proceed. The MCP tools stay locked until you answer.

A record for every decision

Each answer becomes an AI usage record: who, when, which repository, which model, which data, which outcome. Markdown for people, JSON Lines for tools, stored on your computer and given to you in the conversation.

Change audit reports

For any range or environment: who wrote each change, who integrated and approved it, whether it went through a pull request, its risk and where it runs, sealed with a SHA-256 fingerprint. HTML, Markdown, CSV or JSON.

Secure by design

Read-only by default; it writes only on an explicit command (fetch, tag, pull request). Credentials in the system keychain. No mergecat server, no telemetry: your history stays on your machine and your hosting service.
